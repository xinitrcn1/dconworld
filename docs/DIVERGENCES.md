# Divergences from vanilla

Where rwcpp's creature ports differ from Rain World (Downpour and the
Watcher on, no Remix unless a section says otherwise). The AIs of the
kinds below are simplified on purpose: they keep the behaviour a player
notices (what it hunts, flees, how it moves, how it reacts to the slugcat
and its weapons) and leave out the rest.

Shared by every kind ported here:
- Death pits (Creature.Update): a creature that isn't a slugcat, with its first chunk down at the bottom of a room without water (pinned at -restrictInRoomRange), and that can't fly (CreatureTemplate.canFly: centiwings, squidcadas, noodleflies, vultures, overseers, sky whales; those only when stunned or dead) dies and is destroyed. The destroy waits for the end of the rooms' parallel pass. Slugcats keep their own handling.
- Rope-like parts drawn on the GPU (sprites::add_rope: the daddy long legs' tubes, the pole plant's stalk) curve between their points (a cubic Bezier through the neighbours' directions) instead of vanilla's straight TriangleMesh segments.
- Each kind's world file name is read by spawns.cpp and it spawns from its
  den like the rest; `--creature <WorldFileName>,x,y` spawns one by hand.
- The relationships (relationships.cpp) are replayed from StaticWorld.cs,
  including the rows and columns of these kinds.
- Sounds that have no id in sound_types.hpp yet are silent (noted per kind).
- Worm grass isn't a creature here either: like vanilla it's a system of its
  room (patches and worms), with no creature payload.
- Random numbers: no generator is stored anywhere. Every roll is a pure function of the world's seed (set when the world is made, never changed), the tick, the ids of what rolls (dcon ids' `.value`), the call site and which draw of that call it is (`rw::random`, docs/PIPELINE.md). So the rolls differ from UnityEngine.Random's sequence everywhere, on purpose: no roll depends on serial state, and any object, lane or thread can roll in any order. What must come out the same whenever it's rolled (a creature's looks, a shape drawn every frame, the layouts made at a room's load) rolls with tick 0. The audio thread keys its rolls by the sounds it has started, its own clock.
- The rot bulbs' light fading and eye focus (DaddyCorruption.Bulb.Update's) run for every bulb at once before the rooms update (rot::update_attention, docs/PIPELINE.md), so they see last tick's look direction and light: whatever turned an eye or lit a bulb this tick shows in its focus a tick later. Only the drawing reads them.
- Item counters (Weapon.vibrate, PlayerCarryableItem.forbiddenToPlayer) tick down in one pass over every item after the rooms' update (items::update_counters, docs/PIPELINE.md) instead of in each item's update. Everything that sets them runs before that point except Spear.Update's 10 ticks of vibration when it lodges in a wall, which now shakes for 9.
- Floating point: the build targets the host CPU (`RWCPP_NATIVE`, `-march=native`), where GCC fuses `a * b + c` into FMA instructions, so results differ from vanilla's C# in the last bits. A given build is still deterministic: one thread and every thread agree.


## Level rendering (LevelColor, Decal, LightSource)

- The level is drawn in passes instead of once over the sprites: depths 5+ behind the sprites, depths 0..4 over them, then (after the sprites) the sunlit pixels a sprite shades. The sprites are read from the frame's alpha (the level pipelines don't write it, so it holds the sprites' coverage) grabbed after them, standing in for vanilla's GrabPass; behind a semi-transparent sprite the level still shows, where vanilla's goes transparent. The background container's sand and plants count as sprites, like vanilla's.
- `_NoiseTex` and `_NoiseTex2` are hashed value noise in the shaders (level_common.glsl), so the cloud shadows, the grime shimmer and the decals' drips have vanilla's formulas but not its noise image's shapes.
- Not ported: the ripple, heat and melt variants, Watcher terrain (`_SlopedTerrainMask`), snow, the colour adjustment effects (darkness, contrast, saturation, hue, brightness), the wet terrain displacement, lightning, the ghost mode's `_light` and clouds speed. `_SwarmRoom` is always 1. RoomCamera's palette fades are the existing day/rain toggle.
- `_light` and LightSource's fadeWithSun use the room's region cycle's ShaderLight (rain::shader_light, CycleStartUp 1); a room of no region has full sun.
- Lights: the placed LightSources, the LightFixtures (Redlight, RedSubmersible, ZapCoilLight, HolyFire with its flames, SlimeMoldLight, DeepProcessingLight, GlowWeedLight; not SSLightRod), and the LightSources of lantern mice, centipedes (the shock's flash, the aquapede's Glower), lizards' heads, miros birds' feet, the miros vulture's laser, warp tears, flare bombs, lilly pucks on their stalks, and the ExplosionLights of bombs, fire eggs and the vulture's laser, each with its UnderWaterLight sprite in a room with water. The fixtures' and lights' per tick rolls (Redlight's flicker, HolyFire's wander, the flashes' strength) are hashes of the tick and the light, not vanilla's state: HolyFire's lights ease between targets re-rolled every 5 ticks, its flames are stepped from their birth tick each frame. The aquapede's Glower radius follows its alpha (300 at 0.5). ElectricPower is 1 (no BrokenZeroG power cuts, no filtration), a ZapCoilLight's coil is always on (and Submerged Superstructure's aren't off before Moon's heart is back). Not ported: the slugcat's glow and stomach light (`glowing` and swallowed glowing items aren't), the gates' heater lights, Outer Expanse's worm grass light, the oracles', the overseers' holograms, and the LightSources of creatures and items not in rwcpp. A lilly puck off its stalk is dark (no lightFade), its colour is the drawn disc's (no flowerColor). The flare bomb burns as its flash (the item is gone): its LightSource's 60 ticks are a particle at the blast. No darkenLightsFactor, no LightSourceExposure (creatures' visibility ignores lights). nightLight lights fade in with the DayNight effect from the room's own Effects line (templates' effects aren't read); the blink runs on the world tick rather than the room's syncTicker. Environment coloured lights take the colour of the camera image the level shows at the light, every frame.
- The Watcher's sand is rwcpp's 2D dunes (a front face and a top going back), coloured with SlopedTerrainSurface's model: the room's TerrainPalette (and its _rain variant with the night toggle), the sun along lightAngle with `_light`, the clouds' and the sprites' shadows, wetness and the light filtering through water, fog and the sky fade, TerrainLight and TerrainGrain, and up to 16 of the room's LightSources (vanilla's point light loop; Environment coloured ones lit white). Not ported: the sand normal map (`_SandNormals`, TerrainWaves), the stains, rubble, goo and the TerrainFadePalette; the level editor's sun lightmap (`_SlopedTerrainMask`) is whether the level behind the sand is sunlit or sky. The sand is drawn after the sprites (they stay in front of it), hidden where the level is nearer than its depth (layer 6 at the front); the LightSources' own sprites don't treat it as terrain. Without a TerrainPalette it takes the level palette's lit and shaded colours.
- Water keeps rwcpp's own look (the refracted scene, the caustics, the 3D surface band, not DeepWater/WaterSurface), but its colours are the palette's (not its rain half) and the room's light dims the surface's shine and the caustics: the palette's darkness, the clouds and the coming rain (`_light`).
- SandPuffSpawner: the slugcats' chunks on the sand (not on tiles) kick up SandPuffs (landing harder than 5, or one time in ten walking faster than 3), drawn with the Spores shader in the terrain palette's light or dark dust colour (lit: the palette's darkness under 0.5; LitAtCoordinate isn't read). Its rolls are the room's own stream.
- Sandstorm (DangerType Sandstorm, SurfaceSandstorm): only drawn (the Sandstorm shader, its surface mask from the tiles like GenerateSurfaceMask); its wind is stepped in the drawing with hashed rolls. Not ported: pushing objects, the lethality, the screen shake, the wind/sand/rumble loops. Its GlobalIntensity comes with the region's cycle end, which a region where it never rains never reaches (WORA): there only the surface storm blows, fading before the cycle restarts; F6's storm brings the full storm. The noise and clouds textures are hashed value noise.
- A decal or light over several overlapping camera images paints each pixel from the one the level shows there (the last drawn), as vanilla only ever has one camera image. CustomDecal's per vertex rolls (UnityEngine.Random seeded by the corners) are a hash.

## Noodlefly (BigNeedleWorm, SmallNeedleWorm)

- No sounds: none of the noodlefly sound ids (trumpets, wing loops, attack, impale, bounce) are in sound_types.hpp. The cries (SmallCry, BigCry, the young's screams) only show as the snout's quiver (`screaming`).
- The AI is simplified to its core: utility comparison of threat, prey (the adult) and rain; the StuckTracker module (GetUnstuck behaviour) isn't ported, the body's own stuckAtSamePos / reallyStuckAtSamePos unsticking is. No safari control, no MSC licking, no arena "evil AI".
- Migration: no followCreature / quickMoveToExit / OffscreenSuperSpeed (no abstract layer); idle adults roll MigrationBehaviorRoll's InverseLerp(1900, 3200, inRoomCounter) / 6 into migrate::abstract_behavior; confirmedNarrowRooms (rooms it learns to dislike) isn't kept.
- The young's mother is the closest live adult of its own room (re-picked when she dies or leaves), not NeedleWormAbstractAI.FindMotherInRoom's den-based pick that persists across rooms; the young doesn't follow her to other rooms. No shelter egg imprinting (NeedleEgg), no imprinting on the slugcat.
- SocialEventRecognizer: the attacker of a noodlefly (or of a young, for its mother) is a noodlefly whose stab hit it, else the closest slugcat in the room (weapons don't carry their thrower here). The event is "lethal" for damage >= 0.5, "killing" when it died of it; recipients don't need to have seen the attacker. The adult's SocialMemory keeps 4 creatures.
- CreatureLooker simplified: it looks at keepCloseToCreature, else its focus creature (no interest scoring over every tracked creature).
- Pathing: one distance field per destination (pathing::field_to with `needle_worm::fly`: Air at 1 and CicadaPather's open air cost standing in for TravelPreference's wall cost; the +300 for stepping into a narrow space isn't there). FollowPath's look-ahead walks the field. Shortcut connections aren't followed by the body (dens.hpp pulls it into pipes); reachability (CoordinateReachableAndGetbackable) is judged by the field to its current destination. smoothedFloorAltitude is pathing::floor_altitude (not smoothed); MinFlyHeight's worm grass rule (16 over worm grass) isn't there.
- The tail segments' terrain collision is a simplified per axis point collision (SharedPhysics.VerticalCollision/HorizontalCollision, no slopes). Wind isn't applied.
- The fang's trace (TraceProjectileAgainstBodyChunks) is a line-circle test over the room's creatures (not leeches, not the young); daddy tentacles can't be impaled. Impaled chunks have no rotationChunk term. No WaterDrip sparks or screen shake on hitting terrain.
- UtilityComparer's continuation bonus (1.1) is applied to the current behaviour's utility; the prey tracker's FloatTweenBasic smoothing is a lerp of 0.15 a tick.
- The young screams and dies when anything holds it (no Saint exception).
- Eating: a slugcat can pick up a young one handed, carry it through pipes, toss it, and eat it in 5 bites for 2 pips (items.cpp/shortcuts.cpp wired like the baby centipede); the Saint is stunned instead. No Gourmand/Spearmaster specifics beyond what items.cpp already does.
- Graphics: the CicadaWing shader isn't ported: the CentipedeWing quads use vanilla's vertex colours with alpha (0.5 tips, 0.8 roots). DrawSprites' Random roll for the screaming body's swelling is a hash. The MudOverlay (IMuddableGraphics) and culling aren't ported.

## Pole plant (PoleMimic)

- No AI in vanilla either (CreatureTemplate.AI false); safari control (InputCircularRegion), Downpour's LickedByPlayer and the dev tools' "b" pull are not ported.
- Initiate's walk along the line is the story session's 100 tiles; the spawn data's own number (an integer in the world file's creature flags) isn't read.
- A pole plant made away from a pipe (`--creature PoleMimic,x,y`, the fuzz/bench spawns) roots in the terrain closest to where it is made (below first, then left, right, up, within 10 tiles), the stalk standing away from it; vanilla only places one at a den node. Such a plant has no den: withdrawing (hurt or dead) it is destroyed (vanilla's non-den case), and prey it pulls to its root stays pinned there instead of being dragged into a pipe.
- World spawns: the plant is laid out as a pole (vanilla's PlaceInRoom/Initiate, mimicking) the first time its den lets it out; spawns.cpp sets its den wait to 0 (vanilla's remainInDenCounter = 0), but it still travels the den pipe for a moment, so it appears a second or so after the world starts instead of standing there from the start. Later trips out of its den are SpitOutOfShortCut (awake, not mimicking), as vanilla.
- Dragging prey into the den goes through dens.hpp's after_update (going_home with the den entrance as the travel tile): the prey is eaten by the shared den code. A dead plant that has withdrawn is destroyed (ShortcutHandler's MMF behaviour for a dead pole plant) rather than squeezing into the pipe.
- Player.GraspWiggle is a Remix option: a held or stuck slugcat can't wiggle free (escape by hurting the plant: any hit makes it let go, PoleMimic.Stun).
- HitByWeapon's anger is applied to stab and blunt damage (the ported weapons), not explosions or shocks.
- Projectiles hit the stalk (PoleMimic's appendage: root, then the tentacle chunks) through a pole_mimic::trace_stalk hook in creature::trace; the hit pushes the two tentacle chunks around it (ApplyForceOnAppendage).
- Climbing: the stalk is a vine of rot::vines (ClimbableVinesSystem), with the player's vine position carrying the plant (`player.vine_mimic`); VineBeingClimbedOn and VineCurrentlyClimbable are called as in Player.cs. Zero gravity vine grabbing isn't ported (as for the rot vines).
- The "huntChunk in another collision layer" manual Collide only covers leeches (the only other collision layer here).
- Sounds: Pole_Mimic_Stick, Pole_Mimic_Unstick, Pole_Mimic_Grab_Player, Pole_Mimic_Grab_Creature, Player_Grab_Pole_Mimic and Pole_Mimic_Movement_LOOP have no sound ids in sound_types.hpp: skipped.
- Graphics: the stalk's TriangleMesh is the shared GPU rope (sprites::add_rope, vertex pulled): the same points and alternating half widths, curved between the rope segments instead of vanilla's overlapping straight quads. The shadow mesh (sprite 1, in the Shadows container) isn't drawn (the renderer has no shadow layer). The reveal's "first reveals" (PoleMimicEverSeen in the save) is kept per plant, not per save file. The rolls of the leaves' cosmetics use the creature's graphics_random; the leaves' ruffle when a chunk sticks only happens once a camera has made the graphics module (vanilla's module always exists).
- The tentacle's Visual and Rope.Update use the room's terrain proximity map to skip ray traces that can't hit terrain (same results, as daddy_tentacle.cpp).

## Monster kelp (TentaclePlant)

- The tentacle is kept with the plant (data object properties), not as a creature_part: there is always exactly one, with no stun or health of its own.
- Tentacle.cs is mirrored from daddy_tentacle.cpp's port (same shortcuts: the terrain-proximity fast paths for Visual and Rope.Update, the heap-ordered FindTentaclePath), with the plant's TentacleProps (rope, shorten, goal attraction 0.5/0.05, align 0.05, backtrack 2.2, velocity cap 12).
- AI: one shared tracker with up to ten representations instead of vanilla's single one. Prey and threat modules are capped at one, as in vanilla. Anything unseen for 30 ticks is forgotten, so a creature that keeps still is lost as in vanilla. Vision is movement-based from a random tentacle chunk (visualRadius 400, movementBasedVision 1). The water check is an approximation: being submerged on one side and not the other costs a full point.
- TentaclePlantAI's item interest is not ported: the plant does not reach for moving or thrown items and never grabs items, only creatures. preyInterest only mattered against itemInterest, so it is gone too.
- Remix (MMF) behaviour is used: any Violence makes it let go. A hurt plant may let go of a non-slugcat (2%/tick). Player.GraspWiggle is treated as off, so a held slugcat can't wiggle free; it needs to hit or stun the plant.
- Safari control, LickedByPlayer (Saint's tongue), Yellow's (Monk's) easier grab and devtools input are left out.
- Going home uses dens.cpp: the plant sets going_home/travel_tile to its den entrance and dens::after_update pulls it in, eating what it holds on the way in. remainInDenCounter is set to 1200 after a catch and 400 after a threat. The vanilla 160 for items does not apply because it never holds items.
- A plant rooted somewhere other than a den entrance (the --creature CLI, the selftest spawns) treats the solid tile next to it as its den entrance: floor first, then walls, then ceiling, else the floor below. It eats a catch in place (killed, a slugcat marked eaten, a creature destroyed). After a threat it stays drawn in for 400 ticks instead of going into a den. Dead or drawn in while hurt, it is destroyed, like vanilla's non-den case. A dead plant with a den also vanishes once drawn in, instead of going into the den as a corpse (corpses can't enter dens here).
- No rain behaviour: like vanilla's TentaclePlantAI, it doesn't hide from the rain.
- World spawns: spawns.cpp puts it in its den for 100–1000 ticks like every kind. Vanilla's AbstractCreature sets remainInDenCounter = 0 for TentaclePlant, so vanilla plants come out at once.
- No sounds: Tentacle_Plant_Move_LOOP, Tentacle_Plant_Shake_LOOP, Tentacle_Plant_Init_Thrust and Tentacle_Plant_Grab_Slugcat/Other have no sound ids in sound_types.hpp.
- Weapons hit the tentacle as an appendage via creature::trace (a crossing of the tentacle's chunk-to-chunk segments). The hit goes to the tip chunk as momentum instead of through ApplyForceOnAppendage along the tentacle. Spears (damage type stab) get the +50 stun bonus.
- Graphics: TentaclePlantGraphics is ported (the 40-segment rope tube, 50 danglers seeded from the creature's seed with UnityEngine.Random's algorithm, their physics and draw order). Divergences: the JaggedSquare and TentaclePlant shaders are drawn as flat colours, and sprite alphas are ignored. The region's kelpColor and the palette-13 colour variant aren't available, so the default formula is always used. Room.Darkness(rootPos) is approximated by the palette's darkness. No culling. The dangler layout is built the first time a camera looks at the plant.

## Jetfish

- No Remix (MMF) branches: the old swim speeds (Swim(1), 1.8 base swim speed, 1.2 for a heavy rider), no personality energy, no GoThroughFloors/terrainSqueeze changes while ridden, no gift (DangleFruit offering) handling. The MoreSlugcats 0.9 rider damping for bodyWeightFac >= 1.3 is kept.
- JetFishAI simplified: no StuckTracker (no GetUnstuck behaviour, no stuck shaking, no squeezing into a nearby shortcut when stuck), no GoToFood (fruit: DangleFruit, SwollenWaterNut, LillyPuck, GlowWeed never meet creatures here), no froot returned to the den, no NPCTransportation, no safari control. Idle trips to other rooms (migrate.hpp) are taken only when the exit is reachable by swimming.
- UtilityComparer has no smoothing (vanilla has none for these modules either); ties pick threat, then prey, then rain.
- SocialMemory keeps 4 creatures (the slugcats it met); a new one replaces the weakest like. No community influence (CreatureCommunities) and no InfluenceLike/TempLike.
- PathFinder: a flow field to the destination (pathing.hpp, `jetfish::swim`: water tiles only) instead of FishPather; CoordinateReachableAndGetbackable is a field from the fish's own tile. FishPather's OutOfElement isn't ported.
- It never drowns nor suffocates out of the water (vanilla has neither for JetFish): it just flops and hops on land.
- No sounds: Jet_Fish_Water_Jet_LOOP, On_Land_Jump, Ram_Creature, Grab_NPC/Grab_Player, Light/Heavy_Terrain_Impact are missing from sound_types.hpp.
- No WaterJet particles out of the tentacles while jetting at the surface; the ram's 5 Bubbles are the shared particles::bubbles.
- No eating of a dead jetfish by the slugcat (meatPoints 4): rwcpp has no Player.EatMeatUpdate yet (a generic carcass eating system for every kind would cover it).
- Albino: rolled from the region of the room it is created in (SB or the region's albinos flag), not AbstractCreature's spawner region; no arena 4% albinos.
- The slugcat side shares the cicada's HeavyCarry code: pick up (TwoHands while `grabable`), lay down with pick up, toss with throw, the hands on the fish, the pull 5 + 8.5 apart, following through pipes. Player.DeepSwim/SurfaceSwim skip the slugcat's strokes and set waterFriction 1 while it holds a conscious fish.
- Graphics: culling isn't ported (GraphicsModule.culled); the flipper element sizes (JetFishFlipper0..4 sourcePixelSize) are hard coded from the game's atlas. Faithful otherwise (sprite order, z rotation frames JetFish0..8, eyes, whiskers, tentacles, flippers, albino vertex colours).

## Scavenger (Scavenger, ScavengerElite, ScavengerDisciple)

with this heading change and bullets. Rename the section heading to
`## Scavenger (Scavenger, ScavengerElite, ScavengerDisciple)` and use:
- Two grasps instead of vanilla's four: grasps[0] is the throwing hand and grasps[1] the other one (a spear in it is drawn on the back, like vanilla's grasps 1..3). ArrangeInventory works on those two.
- The AI is a cut-down ScavengerAI. It keeps Idle (IdleScore without tile visibility, exit distances, discomfort or the alreadyIdledAt list; it still follows the more dominant scavengers' idle spots), Flee (ThreatTracker.FleeTo, throwing at the threat 25% of ticks), Attack (CheckThrow/TryThrow; the throwing positions are a simplified SpearThrowPositionScore with no flood fills of either creature's movement area), EscapeRain/Injured (dens::home_tile), Scavenge (weapons only) and Travel (migrate.hpp trips).
- Not ported: trading, gifts, squads and squad leaders, tolls and outposts (GuardOutpostModule), communication and pointing animations, rummaging, the NoiseTracker (Investigate), FindPackLeader, the DiscomfortTracker, ghosting out of rooms, the MSC locust and graffiti logic, safari control.
- The relationship to the slugcat replaces the community reputation and SocialMemory with one `like` per scavenger, shared by every slugcat. It drops when a slugcat's thrown weapon hurts that scavenger (-0.5) or any scavenger in its room (-0.25 for the others). It never recovers. With like >= 0 the slugcat is ignored. Below 0 the scavenger Attacks while it holds a weapon and is Afraid without one.
- The violence types are only Warning and Lethal (and the Disciple's NonLethal; no ForFun). A hostile slugcat gets about 160 seen ticks of Warning first: the throw charge with its shake, plus vanilla's warning throws aimed over the target. After that, or once the slugcat comes within 200 px, the violence is Lethal. Other threats are always Lethal.
- Who hurt it is found by looking for a slugcat's thrown item next to the hit chunk when Violence runs, because rwcpp's Violence gets no source. Slugcat melee and explosions don't count.
- WeaponScore and CollectScore only score the items rwcpp has (spears 3, explosive spear 4/1, rocks 2, bombs 3/0, flare bombs and lily pucks 1). Pearls, food and the other valuables are never collected, so `bringPearlHome` is gone too. The ItemTracker is replaced by scanning the room's visible free items every 40 to 200 ticks.
- Movement follows pathing.hpp's distance field (the Scavenger's resistances: floors and corridors 1, poles 2.5). Its steps are Standard, OpenDiagonal and DropToFloor only. There are no ReachUp, DoubleReachUp, ReachOverGap or SemiDiagonal reach moves, no in-room shortcuts, NPC transportation or region transportation, and no pathingWithExits. Swinging between poles (Swing) only happens along climbable tiles on the path; there is no jump across gaps (the Elite's and the Disciple's jumps are below).
- Swim mode is ported. With no swim pathing (the movement has no `swim`), a scavenger that ends up in deep water just paddles at the surface towards its destination.
- Not ported: FlyingWeapon dodging and blocking incoming weapons (TakeDownIncomingWeapon), MeleeGetFree when grabbed, Collide's push between scavengers (scavenger-on-scavenger collisions go through creature.cpp's collide_kin, which has no scavenger case), the AI's UtilityComparer tweening (utilities are compared raw), the CreatureLooker (it looks at its focus creature, else at its look point), and visionFactor/narrowVision (fixed at 0.75 and 1).
- FastReactionCheck is approximated (`reflexBuildUp >= 1 || 50%`). DLCShared's reaction-skill charge times aren't used: charges last 5 ticks before a melee or mid-range throw.
- Throws use vanilla's speed (max(20, exitThrownModeSpeed + 5) = 35 along the aim). The item starts at the first-frame trace point (main - dir*5) rather than at thrownPos (main + dir*50), so targets close by are still hit by rwcpp's per-frame trace. thrownPos is still main + dir*50.
- The migrate roll (AbstractBehavior) is made on 0.2% of ticks, not every tick, because migrate.cpp has no Scavenger laziness entry (vanilla's abstractedLaziness is 50) and the shared table was left alone.
- Scavengers use the room's creature dens through dens.hpp like the other kinds (vanilla scavengers don't use creature holes). They drop what they hold when they go into a den. Items they hold through a room-to-room pipe come along when they come out.
- Sounds: none of the Scavenger_* sound ids exist, so the knuckle, grab-pole and grab-terrain sounds are silent. Picking up uses Slugcat_Pick_Up_Spear (as vanilla does) and throws use the slugcat throw sounds.
- Graphics: ScavengerGraphics is ported with its rolls from the seed, in vanilla order: IndividualVariations, GenerateColors, the back cosmetic (HardBackSpikes or WobblyBackTufts with their patterns and scales), the eartlers and the teeth. The body, waist, chest patch, neck, head, eyes, pupils, teeth, eartlers, arms with ScavengerHandA/B, legs and tail are all drawn. Differences:
  - The darkness isn't lowered by light sources (LightSourceExposure).
  - No mud overlay, and no blindness tint on the eyes.
  - HardBackSpikes' per-frame bristle shiver (a DrawSprites random) is left out.
  - The TailSegments' PushOutOfTerrain ignores the base point.
  - The leg's and hand's unused "pixel" sprites aren't drawn.
  - Held items aren't put in the internal sprite containers. They draw where rwcpp's item pass draws them, not between the scavenger's sprites.
  - Where no camera looks, held items sit on the main chunk (vanilla without a graphics module).
- Personality is rolled here from the seed (AbstractCreature.Personality) and kept in the data object. It feeds the combat skills, the colours, the eartler size and the AI.

## Rain deer

- No Remix (MMF) deer behaviour: the vanilla (non-MMF) branches throughout (legs 220 long, antlers Lerp(30, 70, dominance), every-tick wander rolls, no wiggle-to-startle, no deer-control tutorial).
- Deer-to-deer social behaviour left out: no bowing/kneeling to more dominant deer (`Kneeling` is never on), no deer piles (deerPileCounter), no dominance checks for puff balls or rest spots, no TravelPreference resistance near other deer. Deer still push each other through the generic same-kind collision, antlers included (vanilla's antlers don't collide with objects).
- Collisions with the other creatures are the deer's own pass (deer.cpp `collide_others`, body chunks 0-4 only, so the antlers stay out of it); the other kinds' Collide callbacks aren't called for deer contacts.
- Room changes: vanilla deer travel off screen through the side exits (DeerAbstractAI's allowed rooms and nodes). Here a deer never roams between rooms on its own; it goes home through the pipes (dens.hpp) when the rain comes or when hurt (damageGoHome), and travels the pipes (migrate.hpp trip) when a spore cloud in another room of its region calls it.
- Pathing: one distance field (pathing.hpp, Air allowed) with `open_air` 3 standing in for TravelPreference's `|preferredHeight - terrainProximity|` cost; smoothedFloorAltitude is the plain floor altitude; CoordinateReachableAndGetbackable is "reachable from the destination's field". DeerPather's look-ahead along the path is kept.
- StuckTracker: past positions only; GetUnstuckPosCalculator replaced by a random nearby destination.
- ItemTracker: the deer knows the free puff balls of its room it sees (1450 px, line of sight) and remembers where it last saw the one it goes for. Held puff balls are never targets (as in vanilla without Remix), so heldPuffballNotGiven / deniedPuffballs are left out.
- SporePuffVisionObscurer.AttractADeer runs at the burst instead of ~39 ticks later; the cross-room summons picks any deer of the region (not only vanilla's allowed deer rooms) with a trip there, half the time even when a deer of the room was called (like vanilla).
- The CreatureLooker/Tracker isn't ported: the eyes look at the puff ball it goes for, else the closest slugcat in view within 600 px, else the spore spot.
- PlayerInAntlers: no vulture repelling, no Remix wiggle; riding needs the deer's room to be viewed (vanilla needs its graphics module). The rider's sprites aren't drawn inside the deer's containers (between the far and near horn danglers): the slugcat draws on its own layer. PlayerGraphics' AntlerClimb leg pose (num3 = 2) isn't ported; the hands are (player_graphics.cpp).

## Miros bird

- Solo birds. MirosBirdAbstractAI's flocks, which move birds between rooms together, are not ported. A bird roams its own room's floors and changes rooms only through migrate.hpp's trips and dens.hpp's pipes, like every other creature. Vanilla birds leave rooms over the room's edge (side highways, `forbidStandardShortcutEntry`); `enterRoomHalf`'s push out over the edge is not ported either.
- Idle is a random roam (not vanilla). An idle bird walks to a random reachable floor spot with open space above it, lingers there, and then picks another (`idle_pos`/`idle_counter`). Vanilla's idle bird heads for its den and lets the flock's abstract AI move it about.
- Simplified AI. It keeps MirosBirdAI's utility comparison of threat (0.9), prey (0.5) and rain (0.9), with no smoothing or continuation bonus. Flee goes through ThreatTracker.FleeTo, Hunt runs at the prey's best-guess tile, EscapeRain and ReturnPrey head for the den. GetUnstuck (StuckTracker and its unstuck position) is left out, and the body's stuck shake is what frees it.
- CreatureLooker is reduced to looking at the focus creature: the prey while hunting, the threat while fleeing, as long as it is still tracked. With no focus it looks ahead along its move direction. Vanilla's interest-based switching between tracked creatures is gone.
- Pathing runs over the floor tiles (the `walker` movement: floors plus drops), starting from the floor tile under the body, and the body is steered at points 80 px (`body_lift`) above the path. Vanilla paths through Air, and TravelPreference keeps the path about five tiles above the floor. FollowPath's fallbacks (offset start tiles, picking the most accessible neighbour) are reduced to trying the floor under the other chunks. Low tunnels count as floor, so a bird may try to walk into spaces too small for its body.
- "At the destination" also counts when the floor tile under the body is within 1 tile of the destination, or within 3 tiles when the bird is heading for a pipe. When heading for a pipe, the body is aimed at the pipe tile itself, so it bends down into floor-level entrances that the shared den code pulls it into.
- The beak cycle is ported as in vanilla. The jaw opens and slams shut on its own, and a slam bites whatever it wants to bite in front of the beak (not miros birds, not small creatures). The bite is Grab plus Violence(1.2, Bite), which kills a slugcat. Grab shareability isn't checked. Biting a daddy's tentacles doesn't stun them.
- No jump of its own. Vanilla MirosBird has none either: its leaps come from the run-mode leg springs, which are ported.
- No sounds. The Miros_* sound ids (piston impacts and scrapes, beak snaps, terrain impacts) are not in sound_types.hpp. The legs' InGameNoises are not ported.
- Spark particles from the feet use the shared particle kind, with approximate lifetimes, and only in viewed rooms. They roll from graphics_random.
- The neck is vanilla's tile Tentacle without a rope (the neck's tProps.rope is off anyway). Its segment and grab-path lists are capped at 64 tiles each.
- Graphics: the feet's LightSources (the spark glow in dark rooms) are not ported. Room.Darkness for the eye trail is the palette's darkness, ignoring light sources. Blinded eye flicker and culling are not ported.
- Safari control, the dev-tool keys and `iVars.fatness` (unused by the graphics) are left out.

## Overseer

- No abstract layer. The overseers of WorldLoader.GeneratePopulation are put straight into rooms at world load (the offscreen den's Roam pick: the best of 20 random rooms of the region by size, halved per overseer already there), with the spawn chance taken as at cycle 21 (no early-cycle ramp). From then on the realized AI runs OverseerAbstractAI's parts itself: target creature picking and its counter, Roam to a neighbouring room, following the target to its room (any room of the region), and the zip out of the room when the destination is elsewhere. AbstractBehavior(1) runs twice a tick, as vanilla's OverseerAI.Update calls it.
- Room changes are teleports, as in vanilla (CreatureTeleportOutOfRoom / TeleportingIntoRoom), done as a transaction at the end of the tick. Without world map positions, InterRoomZipPath heads for the exit leading to the other room (pushed 2000 px out), or a random direction 2000 px out when no exit leads there.
- The offscreen den is the overseer hidden in its wall. Before the rain (TimeUntilRain < 4800, SS/RM/DM and never-raining regions excepted) it rolls 1/120 a tick to zip into the wall and stay hidden there: not drawn, not hittable, ignored by the other overseers, though other creatures' trackers can still see it. Once the rain is far off again (the next cycle) it Roams out to a room of its region as from the den.
- The zip pathing matrix isn't kept. It is worked out in full (same costs, same 30 tile window, same random factor) when a zip path is needed. How far UpdateZipMatrix has spread (8 or 16 cells times 2 + 1 a tick) is estimated by counting a ring around the centre as filled once that many cells could have been. AItile.visibility (LookAtAirPosScore) is replaced by the tile's terrain proximity x 40.
- The player guide is simplified. There is one for the whole world (white, yellow, inv and Spearmaster in SU_C04, Gourmand in OE; none for Hunter, Saint, the Watcher, Artificer or Rivulet, whose conditions depend on the save). It follows the first non-NPC slugcat across regions while goToPlayer is on. PlayerGuideUpdate runs with likesPlayer 0 and handHolding 0.5. There is no like or anger bookkeeping, no going away because of scavengers, and no respawn after it dies. Any hit while it is coming quarters its counter (the thrower isn't known). Weapons flying at it don't shorten the counter.
- Holograms (shelter, food and danger pointers, advertisements), the tutorial behaviour, the communication module, safari control, sandbox cursors and moon helpers are not ported. The overseer is never in Projecting mode.
- No OverseerCarcass. A killed overseer flickers out over 6 ticks and is destroyed, leaving no item (vanilla leaves a one-handed, inedible carcass). The overseer itself is neither grabbable nor edible, as in vanilla (it is destroyed before it could be dragged).
- Spears can't stick in it (SpearStick false, as vanilla). Hits use Creature.Violence with HealthState; a spear kills it (damage 1 / 0.9 > instantDeathDamageLimit). Stuns have no effect on it (vanilla's Overseer.Update ignores stun as well).
- An overseer can "converse" with itself, like vanilla's (the random pick includes itself). Mycelia connections between conversing overseers aren't made, and neither are CoralNeuronSystem links.
- HowInterestingIsCreature takes the story karma for every slugcat, and treats a creature in another room as alive. SeededRandom(seed + seed) uses the overseer's seed plus the other creature's index. There is no scavenger outpost or trader check in RoomAllowed, and shelters are never allowed. lookAtFlyingWeapon is the last notified position of the weapon, moved along its velocity until it stops being notified.
- Graphics: OverseerZip, FlatLight and Hologram shaders are drawn as plain alpha colours; the glow is a 50 px soft disc. Sprites draw in vanilla order, but in one layer (no Items/Foreground split). The mesh and mycelia are drawn twice, as vanilla draws them in both containers. totalLength is measured in the graphics update rather than in DrawSprites. The OverseerEffect glows of a dying overseer are not ported (the sparks are); the death ShockWave is a flash particle. The graphics module is only reset or emerged where a camera looks (came_into_view resets it otherwise).
- No sounds: no Overseer_* ids in sound_types.hpp (zip loop, emerge, withdraw, death).

## Snail (Snail)

- Click: the pop stuns (Player.Stun, Creature.Stun: 60 * closeness) and flings every creature's chunk (slugcats and leeches included) and every item's in sight within 60 * size + radii, 1 + 4.5 * both submersions times as far and as hard under the water; a leech it catches dies (1 in 30, or touching the shell), else is stunned 800-900 * closeness * submersion; Leech.HeardSnailClick's push away is done, the leeches' afraidOfPositions aren't ported. No ShockWave visual; the WaterDrips/Bubbles are the shared particles; the water surface takes waterObject.Explosion as water::disturb. The Saint's SaintStagger(1500) is a plain stun; no Remix stun increase (cfgIncreaseStuns); no ripple layers (the Watcher's): everything in the room is in reach. Barnacles in reach lose their shell (Barnacle.LoseShell).
- The Inv's exploding snails in DS (and Room.Update's snail spawning there) aren't ported.
- Triggers kept: clickCounter charge while scared (> 0.3), +0.025 per tick touching a creature (the slugcat included), 0.92 on a light hit or a landing over 5, `triggered` on a hit of 0.1 damage or a stun bonus over 10, a landing over 20, 40 ticks after a slugcat's throw (Player.ThrowObject's triggerTicker); the mini clicks at the rolled miniClicks. Player.JumpOnChunk isn't ported (rwcpp has no jump-off-objects), so jumping on a snail doesn't add 0.8; `snail::jumped_on` is ready for it.
- SnailAI simplified: no TravelPreference (the flow fields are shared, no per creature costs around tracked creatures); TileIdleScore without aimap.Visibility and with the den's ExitDistanceForCreature as tiles to its pipe; CoordinateReachable/PossibleToGetBackFrom are one field from its own tile; CreatureUnease without the AirOnly/WaterOnly thirds. Tracker keeps 10 creatures (vanilla 5). No MMF shortcut shuffle (no Remix). No LickedByPlayer (MSC). Migration (migrate.hpp idle_roll/travel_tile) stands in for the abstract AI's roaming.
- SnailPather: a flow field (pathing.hpp, `snail::crawl`: every surface, poles and under water at 1) instead of SnailPather; its DropToFloor stays the generic drop cost (no Unwanted x100 unless scared/raining, no forced allow except into deep water); the MovementConnection's kind is read off the step (diagonal over a slope tile: Slope/CeilingSlope, else OpenDiagonal; straight down more than a tile: DropToFloor). It doesn't take random shortcuts; dens.hpp takes it into its den's pipe going home before the rain.
- No sounds: Snail_Pop, Warning_Click, Charging_LOOP, Walking_LOOP, Attatch_To_Surface, Emerge_From/Withdraw_In_Shell, Light/Heavy_Terrain_Impact are missing from sound_types.hpp.
- The slugcat side shares the jetfish's HeavyCarry code: pick up (TwoHands, no stun needed), lay down with pick up (no trigger), toss with throw (triggerTicker 40), the hands on the shell, the pull 5 + chunk radius apart, following through pipes. No eating a dead snail (meatPoints 1: no Player.EatMeatUpdate yet); Spearmaster's needle feeding isn't ported.
- Graphics: faithful sprite order and maths (SnailTail, SnailLimb x2, SnailHead, two pixel eyes, SnailShellA/B in the rolled colours, the shake while triggered/charging, the bloat on a click); CreatureLooker is simplified (the tracked creature with the best chance * (1 + unease), re-picked 1 in 30 ticks); the Circle20 wall shadow (Shadows container, shadowExtensionFac) and the mud sprites (IMuddableGraphics) aren't drawn; the shake's UnityEngine.Random is a per tick hash.

## Sky whale (SkyWhale)

- What affects the slugcat is kept: the six heavy chunks (16.7 mass each, 32 to 63 px radius) are in collision layer 1 and shove the slugcat (it can ride on the back, pushed up every tick); Collide never hurts (stunOnCollideVelocity 0); it never attacks (its template Eats the slugcat, but vanilla has no grasps or hunting for it either); it is hurt by Creature.Violence with baseDamageResistance 50 / baseStunResistance 8. A dead or stunned whale drifts (gravity 0, airFriction 0.98).
- Whiskers: the six SkyWhaleWhiskers (not the AltVariant's) are simulated every tick and are climbable vines through rot::vines (the player's vine position carries the whale and the whisker: `player.vine_whisker`), past the 3 forbidden segments, Rad 0.1, Mass 0.2. The segments' terrain collision is a per-tile push back out along the freeing axis (not SharedPhysics' rad 2 collision). CurrentlyClimbable's ripple layer check is always true.
- Nodes: SkyWhalePathfinding placed objects are now parsed into placed_thing (`thing_kind::sky_whale_node`: pos, handle). The node index (the data's third field) isn't used, as in vanilla. At most 32 nodes per room are walked. The used list is a mask in file order, so testNodeIndex cycles in file order rather than the order nodes were used.
- Pathing (SkyWhalePather) simplified: it flies straight at its goal when the line is clear (sight, and 3+ tiles off the terrain every 40 px). Otherwise it follows a pathing field (Air at 1, CicadaPather's open air cost) with vanilla's look-ahead average of up to 8 visible steps. The "within 4 tiles of the terrain is illegal" rule only applies to the idle spots and unstuck goals, not to the field's tiles. CoordinateReachableAndGetbackable is "open tile 4+ off the terrain".
- No abstract layer, and no side exits or offscreen den: the world file's OFFSCREEN whales are made off screen (parked above a room of the region that has nodes; WPTA_D04 is blacklisted, as in BLACKLISTEDSKYS) and fly in over a random side after 100 to 1000 ticks, the AltVariant high up. Leaving (EscapeRain, an idle whale tiring of its room, and a FollowPath whale after the abstract AI's timeToTire of 1700 + 4000*(1-energy), energy rolled at random) means flying out over the nearest side. Once fully past the edge it hides for 400 to 1600 ticks, stays hidden while RainTracker.Utility > 0.01, then flies into a random room of its region that has nodes (its own if there are none). A whale that can't get out within 2400 ticks hides anyway. A realized vanilla whale in a room with nodes never leaves; here it does, standing in for the abstract roaming. Side gates, BLACKLISTEDSIDES, checkRooms and the attraction weights aren't ported. While hidden it gets no physics, no rain, no collisions and no hits, and isn't drawn.
- StuckTracker simplified: "stuck" grows when the head moves under 20 px in 40 ticks. GetUnstuck heads for a random open tile within 12 tiles with vanilla's direct pushes and terrainSqueeze 0.5.
- UtilityComparer: rain (weight 1 in rooms that rain, rain::rains_in) and stuck, each to the power 1.1, without the continuation bonus.
- No safari control, LickedByPlayer, dev-tools "b" pull, LocustSystem interaction, LeviathanWhale or NamedAttr ({NamedAttr:TOPWhale}... are ignored).
- Sounds: Sky_Whale_Cry, Sky_Whale_Click, Sky_Whale_Groan, Sky_Whale_Fin_Flap, Vulture_Jet_Start/Stop have no sound ids: silent.
- GasJet: a stab (its direction taken from the hit's momentum, as the source isn't known) starts one of up to 3 jets on the hit chunk, 80 to 120 ticks of dust particles (bubbles under water) instead of NewVultureSmoke.
- Graphics: SkyWhaleGraphics is ported (layout rolled from the seed like the constructor, sprite order, the two z-sorted containers for far-side fins and whiskers, the palette colours), but the SkyWhaleBody and SkyWhaleCuticle shaders aren't: the body is flat skin colour under the underside, and the armour's cuticle overlay mesh isn't drawn. Marking sizes are the atlas elements' sizes, hardcoded. CreatureLooker is simplified: about every 70 ticks it looks at the closest visible creature or at nothing (no interest scoring by CreatureUnease). No culling. SegmentFromDist is clamped to the last segment (vanilla could index past it).
- Thread safety: the AI never writes another creature's state. The slugcat writes the whiskers from the same room's update. Room changes are deferred (serial). The simulation never reads the graphics data.

## Yeek (Yeek)

- YeekAI simplified: no StuckTracker (no GetUnstuck behaviour, hops or shaking), no Hungry/ReturnFood (fruit never meets creatures here: no fruit scanning, goalFruit, grabbing fruit in Collide, CarryObject, feeding in the den; YeekState's hunger is not kept), no LickedByPlayer, no TravelPreference (the threat's tiles cost nothing extra while fleeing), no `stranded`, no safari control (Act's controlled branch, inputWithDiagonals).
- UtilityComparer: threat and rain only (power 1.1); ties go to the rain.
- The squad: a yeek it tracks is Pack whatever the static table says (vanilla turns the default Afraid into Pack); 8 remembered. SurprisedFearReaction's squad mates take the threat into their tracker on their own next update (vanilla points their ThreatTracker at it at once); the reaction calls MakeCreatureLeaveRoom and YeekCall once rather than once per mate. The relationship's height/distance term (×1 in vanilla, its maxValue is float.MaxValue) is left out.
- IdlePosScore: a tile it can't get back from scores 0 (vanilla only takes a fifth off one it can reach but not get back from, and nothing off an unreachable one); "reachable" is a path field from the yeek's tile (pathing.hpp). A tile 400 tiles or more from the threat is "divided by zero": the best score (as vanilla's Infinity).
- MakeCreatureLeaveRoom: a migrate.hpp trip to a neighbour room (random_move(1)) instead of the ThreatTracker's most attractive exit; when it can't leave, getNewIdlePos waits 200 ticks before trying again (vanilla tries every tick).
- Movement: the path field's next tile stands for the MovementConnection: a drop of 2 tiles or more is DropToFloor/DropToClimb/ReachDown, a diagonal or slope step off a pole is the Slope/OpenDiagonal hop, a narrow space or a pipe entrance is crawling; no ReachOverGap hops (the field has no gap jumps), no NPCTransportation, no shortcuts within the room (pipes are dens.hpp's and migrate.hpp's).
- Climbing and crawling always take the graphics module's way (Climb's and the tunnel's CanAdvanceClimb/AdvanceClimb), with the legs' "both at their goal" approximated by a step every 2 ticks (`climb_step`), so the simulation never depends on whether the room is viewed.
- Sitting still near its idle spot (within 8 tiles) only when the destination isn't a pipe (its den, a trip's exit): vanilla's den/exit coordinates have no tile, so it never settles short of them either.
- Hop/Jump/Act otherwise as vanilla (the fear scaling, the 50/40 caps, the ceiling clearance, the hurt yeek's stun and sideways wobble). The bodyChunkConnection distance is set to 2 held and 10 unconscious and never back to 15, as vanilla.
- Held by a slugcat: as vanilla (stun 0, the light mass, the random hop and the jump-button hop that lifts the slugcat, controlledJumpVelocity). The held yeek is a HeavyCarry TwoHands creature through the jetfish's slugcat-side code (pick up, lay down with pick up, toss with throw, the 5 + rad pull, pipes).
- Meat: Player.CanEatMeat/EatMeatUpdate for a held dead yeek only (Hunter, Artificer, Gourmand, Inv; meatPoints 2: a pip a bite, the Gourmand's and Inv's half pip), with the count of the eating (Player.eatMeat) kept by the yeek; no Slugcat_Eat_Meat sounds, no WaterDrip, no head shake, no Blink, no pyroJumpCooldown check. (A generic carcass eating system for every kind would replace it.)
- Sounds: only Slugcat_Wall_Jump (the hop); YeekCall's Small_Needle_Worm_Little_Trumpet / Big_Needle_Worm_Small_Trumpet and Vulture_Grab_NPC are missing from sound_types.hpp.
- No Winterized look (Saint's blizzard yeeks), no devtools "b" key, no dangerousToPlayer/arena kill scores.
- Graphics: faithful sprite order and colours (feathers, body+tail mesh with the highlighted tail tuft, neck, second tail, legs, head, eyes); the head and legs aren't moved to the Foreground container while climbing; the eye shudder is rolled once a tick, not per frame; the CreatureLooker is the AI's focusCreature or the most threatening creature; the YeekTails (cosmetic only) run in the graphics update with the room's tile push-out instead of SharedPhysics' terrain collision; no culling.

## Vultures (Vulture, KingVulture, MirosVulture)

- One kind with a variant. Vulture.cs, VultureTentacle (the wings: creature_parts) on Tentacle, KingTusks, VultureGraphics/VultureFeather/VultureAppendage ported; VultureAI simplified; VultureAbstractAI, VulturePather's border exits, VultureMask and the smoke systems not ported.
- No sky exits, no offscreen den: vultures roam the open air of their room and change rooms through pipes like everything else (migrate.hpp); vanilla's forbidStandardShortcutEntry is ignored. World file OFFSCREEN vultures get a den in a room of their region with open sky and room in front of the den.
- Taking prey home (or disencouraged), a vulture flies up an open column over the top of the room when there is one; once 200 over the top the prey is eaten (a slugcat eaten like in a den, a creature gone) and the vulture hovers out of sight for 800-2400 ticks before coming back. Without sky it carries the prey into its den. EscapeRain heads for the den (the sky without one: it may then die in the rain up there). Idle, now and then it goes up into the sky for 400-1600 ticks.
- VultureAI: no StuckTracker (GetUnstuck, CloseToGoalButNotSeeingIt), no GoToMask, no creature looker (the head looks at its focus or its destination); the rain is a behavior of its own (vanilla's realized AI leaves it to the abstract AI); the king's prey intensity isn't scaled by the prey's accessibility; story difficulty 0 (slugcat Eats 0.35, +0.15 the king; Monk 0.15).
- SocialMemory: a vulture without its mask attacks every slugcat (vanilla: only the one whose hit knocked it off, the killTag). Attacks: its snap only bites (1.1, deadly), no grab.
- The mask turns any thrown weapon coming at the face head on (vanilla: any source with a direction); a hit of more than 0.9 knocks it off, but no VultureMask item is dropped (needs a new item type: a one chunk Weapon-less PlayerCarryableItem with king/non-king and the seed's colours, VultureMask.cs's drawing, wearable by scavengers; the lizards' fear of masks would read it). Masks are only the protection here.
- The king's harpoon wire is a straight line (vanilla's Rope wraps around corners); a harpoon on a creature another holds exclusively lets go at random only for any other holder; the wire's look moves only where a camera looks.
- The miros vulture fires its laser when hurt whether a camera looks or not (vanilla needs its graphics module); the laser's explosion is the port's Explosion (250 radius, slugcats closer than 150 die), without its light and fragments.
- Thruster smoke is cosmetic (vanilla's NewVultureSmoke pushes nothing either): drawn as dust puffs, bubbles under water. Not drawn: the shadow cast while high over the room (shadowMode), the snapping head's jitter, the Blinded eye colours, the Gourmand dropped more often (not told apart).
- Silent (no samples): every Vulture_*, King_Vulture_*, Miros_* sound, the jet and wing loops.
- Thread safety: the vulture only touches creatures and the slugcats of its own room; graphics-module state is never read by the simulation (take off / fall colour waves are requested through a counter in the body state, which the module consumes).

## Daddy long legs

- The tubes are the shared GPU rope (see above); the bulbs are one quad each whose fragment shader draws the jagged circle (the same rolls and 18 corners as the TriangleMesh fan).
- Hunting is greedier than vanilla's. The PreyTracker keeps everything edible the tentacles feel (up to 16, not 5-7). The current prey, the nearest one (felt ones first), is what the body walks to. Each free tentacle goes for the nearest tracked prey in its reach that no other tentacle is hunting. Vanilla picks one random tracked prey and gives up when that one is out of reach or already hunted. Up to 12 creatures can be digested at once (vanilla's list has no cap, the port had 4), so a long legs over a pack of slugcats reaches for all of them.
- No long legs eats a big creature: the loaches, rain deer, vultures (King and Miros too), miros birds and every long legs variant (`creature::is_big_creature`). Vanilla's table lets the Daddy eat the Brother, and Remix's CheckDaddyConsumption lets the big ones eat smaller long legs. The grandmother is a TerrorLongLegs to the relationship table, and the HunterDaddy is a HunterDaddy (the variant used to be read one template off).
- The body chunks (the core bulbs) weigh 0.1 each instead of vanilla's num5 (up to several each). superSizeMe's lower gravity (0.75) is gone. The heavy bodies of the big variants used to drop through what their tentacles held. The Hunter's two slugcat-dummy chunks keep their 20.

## Lizards

- Lizard.LegsGripping and NoGripCounter don't come from LizardGraphics' legs (vanilla: the limbs' gripCounter when the room has a graphicsModule, 2 and 0 without one). The body keeps its own count every tick (lizard.cpp `update_grip`): each leg runs Limb.FindGrip from its body chunk within the leg's length (jointDist) toward where the body aims its legs, and grips after limbGripDelay ticks in a row with terrain in reach; a leg under half health, swimming or a stunned lizard grips nothing. The simulation never reads graphics state, and a lizard moves, falls and lunges (Lounge needs more than two legs gripping) the same whether a camera looks at it or not. Vanilla's off-screen lizards never lunge; these do.
